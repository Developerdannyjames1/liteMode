Devotional #2

Guided by Grace

Grace That Leads You Forward

" Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path. "

Psalm 119:105

Gallery Moment 1

The Word

Proverbs 3:5–6

Trust in the Lord with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways submit to Him, and He will make your paths straight.

Gallery Moment 2

Reflection

Life is full of choices, turns, and unknowns. But you were never meant to figure it out alone. God's grace is a light that goes before you and a strength that walks beside you.

When you trust Him, even what you can't see becomes a path you can walk with peace.

Gallery Moment 3

The Heart

Grace does not push you.

Grace does not shame you.

Grace does not leave you.

Grace leads you —
gently, faithfully, always.

Gallery Moment 4

Pause & Consider

  • Where do you need God's guidance today?
  • What would change if you truly trusted that His grace is leading you?

Gallery Moment 5

Identity Truth

I walk in the light of His grace.
He goes before me.
He leads me into good.

Gallery Moment 6

Prayer

Lord, thank You for Your amazing grace that leads me every step of the way. Help me to trust You completely, even when I don't understand the path ahead. You are faithful, Your grace is enough, and I will follow You today and always. Amen.

Gallery Moment 7

Carry the Light Forward

As you go today, carry this truth in your heart:

His grace is enough.
His light is guiding you.
