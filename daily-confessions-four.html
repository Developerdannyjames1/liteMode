Daily Confessions

Speaking God's Truth ... Living From His Finished Work

Let the Word of God fill your heart, renew your mind and shape your day. These Spirit-filled confessions will help you stand in what Jesus has already accomplished so you can live in His love, peace, power and victory.

Daily Confession Four

I Walk in God's Wisdom and Peace

His Word Guides Me. His Peace Guards Me. His Wisdom Leads Me.

Scripture Foundation

"If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask of God, who gives to all generously and without reproach, and it will be given to him."

James 1:5 (NKJV)

Speak this confession out loud, slowly and with faith.
Allow God's truth to take root in your heart today.

Today, I Confess ...

  • I seek God's wisdom in every decision.
    James 1:5 | Proverbs 3:5–6
  • The peace of God guards my heart and mind.
    Philippians 4:6–7
  • God's Word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path.
    Psalm 119:105
  • I trust God to lead me and direct my steps.
    Proverbs 16:9
  • I cast my cares on Him, for He cares for me.
    1 Peter 5:7
  • My mind is renewed by the truth of God's Word.
    Romans 12:2
  • I walk in the Spirit and not in the flesh.
    Galatians 5:16
  • God's plans for me are good, and my future is secure.
    Jeremiah 29:11
  • I live in His perfect peace, even in uncertain times.
    Isaiah 26:3
  • Today, I follow His voice with a willing and obedient heart.
    John 10:27

God is my wisdom, my guide and

my peace — today and always!

Let This Truth Shape Your Day

You do not have to figure life out on your own.
God's wisdom is available to you.
His peace is for you.
His guidance is for you.

Speak the truth.
Believe the truth.
Walk in the truth.

"Peace I leave with you, My peace I give to you; not as the world gives do I give to you. Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid."

John 14:27 (NKJV)
