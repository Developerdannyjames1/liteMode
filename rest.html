REST

Come unto Me. I will give you rest.

✿

Life can be loud and heavy.
You were never meant to carry it all.
Jesus invites you into a place where your heart can be quiet,
your mind can let go,
and your soul can breathe.

Come. Breathe. Rest.

“Come unto me,
all ye that labour
and are heavy laden,
and I will give you rest.”
MATTHEW 11:28
❝
In peace I will both lay me down, and sleep:
for thou, LORD, only makest me dwell in safety. PSALM 4:8

Rest Is Not
Wasted Time

Rest restores your body, quiets your mind, and strengthens your spirit. It reminds us that God is God even when we stop working.

“Be still, and know that I am God.”
PSALM 46:10
✿

Position Yourself
for Rest

  • Release the day and let go of worry.
  • Quiet the noise and lower the lights.
  • Breathe slowly and relax your body.
  • Give tomorrow to God.
  • Receive rest—He will give it.
🙏

He Holds
Tomorrow

You don't have to solve everything before you sleep. Place your cares into His hands and rest in His faithfulness.

“I will both lay me down in peace,
and sleep: for thou, LORD, only
makest me dwell in safety.”
PSALM 4:8

Before You Go...

He is with you.

There is no need to carry tomorrow tonight. You are loved. You are held. Let the day end. Let your heart be quiet. Come unto Him. And receive His rest.
