Daily Confessions

✦

Speak the Word.

Renew Your Mind.

Strengthen Your Faith.

God's Word is meant to be spoken with faith. These Spirit-filled confessions will help you agree with what God has already said about you — so you can live in His love, peace, power and victory.

Welcome to the Daily Confessions Page

Each confession is rooted in Scripture and designed to help you stand in what Jesus has already accomplished. Speak them aloud, believe them in your heart, and walk in the truth of God's Word every day.

“

Death and life are in the power of the tongue, and those who love it will eat its fruit.

— Proverbs 18:21

Confessions by Focus

Identity in Christ

Discover who you truly are — a beloved child of God, fully loved, accepted and secure in Him.

Peace and Rest

Walk in God's wisdom and peace. Let His Word guide you and His peace guard your heart and mind.

Healing and Wholeness

Confess the finished work of Christ over your body, soul and spirit. Receive His healing and restoration.

Wisdom and Direction

Seek God's wisdom in every decision. Trust Him to lead you and direct your steps each day.

Provision and Abundance

Declare God's promises over your finances, resources and future. He is your provider and source.

Strength and Courage

Stand in victory as more than a conqueror. Walk in authority, dominion and the power of the Holy Spirit.

How to Use Daily Confessions

Read

Read the confession slowly and with purpose.

›

Speak

Speak the words aloud in faith, declaring God's truth over your life.

›

Believe

Believe that what God has said is true — for you, today.

›

Carry It

Carry the truth with you throughout your day.

›

Walk It Out

Live each day from what God has already said and done.

You don't have to feel it to speak it.
You don't have to see it to believe it.
You only need to agree with God — and He will do the rest.

Carry Truth Every Day

Make these confessions part of your daily rhythm. Download, print, or save them to your journey — and let God's Word shape every moment of your day.
