Why These Stories?

They quiet the mind and soothe the soul.

They help us let go of worry.

They soften the edges of a hard day.

They remind us that we are never alone.

A Moment of Rest

Make your space comfortable.

Dim the lights and turn down the noise.

Take a slow breath.

Give this moment to God.

He is with you.
You can rest in His peace. PSALM 4:8

Tips for a Restful Evening

Create a calm space.
Put distractions away and give yourself permission to be still.

Listen and receive.
Let the story lead you into peace.

End your evening with gratitude.
Thank God for His faithfulness.
