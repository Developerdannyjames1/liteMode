Grow in Faith Every Day

Little Lights Grow

Plant seeds of faith, water them with God's Word, and watch your heart bloom with love for Jesus. Every day is a chance to grow closer to God!

How We Grow

1

Read God's Word

Open your Bible and discover amazing stories about God's love, power, and promises for you.

2

Pray Every Day

Talk to God like a friend. Share your joys, worries, and thank Him for all the good things.

3

Be Kind & Brave

Let God's love shine through your actions. Be kind to others and brave in doing what is right.

4

Remember & Share

Write down what you learn and tell your friends and family about God's amazing love.
