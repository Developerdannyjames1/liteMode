Devotional #4

Steadfast and Sure

The Light That Never Shakes

❧
“
The Lord is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear?
The Lord is the stronghold of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?
Psalm 27:1
“
📖
Gallery Moment 1

The Word

Psalm 27:1

The Lord is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? The Lord is the stronghold of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?

♡
Gallery Moment 2

Reflection

In a world that changes with every wave, God remains the same. His light does not flicker. His promises do not fail. His love does not walk away. He is your steady place when life feels uncertain, and your safe place when storms come.

You can lean into His constancy, because His light is sure, His Word is true, and His presence never leaves you.

♔
Gallery Moment 3

The Heart

He is my strength when I am weak.

He is my peace when I am afraid.

He is my refuge when I feel overwhelmed.

He is my light that never goes out.

I rest in His unshakable love.

⏸
Gallery Moment 4

Pause & Consider

  • What areas of your life need the steady light of God today?
  • How can you remind your heart daily that He is your refuge?
✝
Gallery Moment 5

Identity Truth

I stand in the unshakable light of God.
He is my strength, my refuge,
and my steady place.

🙏
Gallery Moment 6

Prayer

Lord, thank You for being my steady light in every season. When life feels uncertain, help me to run to You. When I am afraid, remind me that You are my refuge. Thank You for staying the same yesterday, today, and forever. I will trust in You and rest in Your light. In Jesus' name, amen.

🕯
Gallery Moment 7

Carry the Light Forward

As you go today, carry this truth in your heart:

His light is always with me.
His love is always for me.
His strength is always enough.
