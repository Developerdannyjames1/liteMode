A Note for Parents

Bedtime is more than the end of a busy day. It's a beautiful opportunity to help your child's heart slow down, their mind settle, and their spirit rest in the truth that God is with them.

Our bedtime fables are created to be more than just stories. They are gentle reminders of God's love, goodness, and faithfulness.

Snuggle close. Take a deep breath. Let's end the day together.

May these stories bring sweet dreams, peaceful hearts, and a night filled with His presence.

♡Tips for a Peaceful Bedtime

Create a Calm Space.
Dim the lights, lower the noise, and make the room cozy.

Keep a Gentle Routine.
A simple rhythm helps little hearts and bodies know it's time to rest.

Share a Story or Scripture.
God's Word and a good story plant seeds of truth.

Pray and Let Go.
Talk to God together and give the day into His hands.

♡A Bedtime Prayer

Dear God,
Thank You for this day.
Thank You for being with me
and for loving me so much.
Please watch over me as I sleep
and help me have sweet dreams.
In Jesus' name,
Amen. — A prayer for little hearts
“ I will both lay me down
in peace, and sleep:
for thou, LORD,
only makest me
dwell in safety. ” PSALM 4:8
