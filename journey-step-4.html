The Problem Christ Solved

Before we can fully grasp who we are, we must understand what separated us — and what it took to bring us back.

The Separation

Sin created a chasm between humanity and God. Not because God turned away, but because holiness cannot coexist with sin. We were separated from the very source of life, love, and identity.

  • Adam and Eve hid from God in shame
  • Humanity has been running ever since
  • Religion tried to bridge the gap — and failed

The Solution

God did not wait for us to clean ourselves up. While we were still sinners, Christ died for us. He became the bridge we could never build. The cross was not Plan B — it was the plan from the beginning.

  • Jesus lived the life we could not live
  • He died the death we deserved
  • He rose again to give us new life

The Story of Separation and Rescue

We Were Alienated

Sin made us strangers to God. We were enemies in our minds, doing evil deeds, far from the relationship we were created for. The gap was too wide for any human effort to close.

We Were Helpless

No amount of good behavior, religious ritual, or self-improvement could erase the debt. The law exposed our sin but could not remove it. We needed someone to step in who was both fully God and fully human.

We Were Reconciled

Through Christ's blood, God made peace with us. He did not just forgive — He removed the record of debt. He reconciled us to Himself, not counting our sins against us. The wall came down.

"For if, while we were God's enemies, we were reconciled to him through the death of his Son, how much more, having been reconciled, shall we be saved through his life!"

Romans 5:10

Personal Reflection

Let the weight of this truth settle in your heart. God pursued you while you were still running.

1. The Before Picture

Before Christ, what were you trying to use to fill the gap between you and God? Success? Religion? Relationships? Distractions?

2. The Rescue

How does it change your view of God to know He initiated the rescue while you were still a stranger to Him?

3. The Response

What is one area of your life where you are still trying to earn what Christ has already given you for free?

Before You Continue
