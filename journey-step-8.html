Identity

Who You Are In Christ

Your identity is not based on your performance, your past, or what others think. It is based solely on what God has done for you through Christ. You are complete in Him.

  • You are a child of God (John 1:12)
  • You are chosen, holy, and dearly loved (Colossians 3:12)
  • You are seated with Christ in heavenly places (Ephesians 2:6)
  • You are more than a conqueror (Romans 8:37)

Living Out Your Identity

Knowing who you are is the first step. Now you get to live it out — every single day. Let your identity in Christ shape your thoughts, words, and actions.

  • Walk in the truth of who God says you are
  • Reject the lies that contradict your identity
  • Share your identity with others — you are a light
