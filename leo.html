Meet Leo Rivera

The Guardian

Leo is loyal, courageous, and always ready to protect. He believes in doing what's right, standing up for others, and keeping the team safe. But Leo is also learning that true strength includes trusting others to stand beside him.

Leo's Story

Leo has a big heart and a strong sense of responsibility. At home and on the team, he looks out for others. Sometimes Leo feels like he has to be the strongest one, the one who always has the answers, or the one who can handle everything.

But as the adventures unfold, Leo discovers that courage doesn't mean facing the journey alone. It means trusting his friends, listening to wisdom, and knowing when to ask for help.
