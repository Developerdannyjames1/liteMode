Deep Sleep

Let the Day Go.
Let His Word Carry You into Rest.

♡

Deep Sleep is a peaceful experience of soothing Scriptures gently recited over soft music and the calming sound of flowing water.

Let the promises of God quiet your heart, calm your thoughts, and carry you into a place of deep, peaceful rest.

Sound of Water
Soothing Music
❝ In peace I will both lay me down, and sleep:
for thou, LORD, only makest me dwell in safety. PSALM 4:8

About This Experience

Designed to help you slow down, release the noise of the day, and rest in the unchanging promises of God's Word.

Soothing Scriptures

Encouraging truth from God's Word to quiet your heart.

Soft Music

Gentle melodies created to help your mind and body relax.

Sound of Flowing Water

The calming sound of water to help you let go and be still.

♡

You don't have to figure anything out.
You don't have to make anything happen.
Just listen. Receive. Rest.

Prepare for Deep Sleep

Get Comfortable

Lie down or sit in a comfortable place.

Dim the lights

Create a quiet, peaceful space.

Put distractions away

Silence your phone and turn off screens.

Breathe slowly

Take a deep breath and let go.

♡ Find a quiet place and allow God's Word to wash over you. He is with you. You are safe. You can rest.

Why God's Word?

His Word has the power to calm our hearts, renew our minds, and speak peace over every part of our being.

“So then faith cometh by hearing,
and hearing by the word of God.”
ROMANS 10:17

Benefits of Rest

Refreshes your body
Rest allows your body to recover and be renewed.

Calms your mind
Quieting your thoughts reduces stress and worry.

Strengthens your spirit
Time spent with God renews your faith and hope.

Prepares you for tomorrow
Rest gives you the strength and clarity you need for the new day.

Before You Go...

Place whatever you're carrying into the hands of your loving Father.

He is with you.
He is faithful.
You can rest in His peace.

“Be still, and know that I am God.”
PSALM 46:10
