Gentle Habits for Deeper Rest

Small shifts that help your body and soul prepare for sleep.

🕯️

Dim the Lights

Lower your lights 30 minutes before bed. Let your body know the day is ending and peace is coming.

📖

Read Scripture

Even one verse can anchor your heart. Let God's Word be the last thing your mind holds tonight.

🙏

Gratitude Pause

Name three things you're thankful for. Gratitude quiets the anxious mind like nothing else.

A Gentle Path

Your Bedtime Routine

A simple flow to guide your heart from busy to rest.

1
30 min before bed

Disconnect & Dim

Put your phone away. Lower the lights. Let your eyes and mind begin to soften into the evening.

2
20 min before bed

Breathe & Release

Take five slow, deep breaths. With each exhale, imagine letting go of one worry from the day.

3
10 min before bed

Listen & Pray

Press play on tonight's prayer. Let the words wash over you like a gentle lullaby for your soul.

4
As you drift

Surrender to Sleep

Let the prayer loop softly. You don't have to stay awake for it. God is listening even as you sleep.

In peace I will lie down and sleep, for you alone, Lord, make me dwell in safety.
