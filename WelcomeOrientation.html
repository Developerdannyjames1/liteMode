Welcome Orientation

Before we begin this journey together, let's set our hearts in the right place. God has already prepared a path for you.

You Are Not Here By Accident

Every step of this journey has been designed to help you discover who you truly are in Christ. Not who the world says you are. Not who your past says you are. But who God has already declared you to be.

What Is The Light Journey?

The Light Journey is an 8-step guided experience that walks you through the foundations of your identity in Christ. Each step builds upon the last, creating a clear path from confusion to confidence.

  • Short, focused readings you can complete in 10-15 minutes
  • Simple reflection questions to help truth sink deep
  • One clear action step before moving forward

How This Works

You cannot skip steps. Each page has a simple task you must complete before the "Continue" button unlocks. This isn't about checking boxes — it's about letting truth transform you.

  • Read the welcome message carefully
  • Check off each understanding below
  • Click "Proceed to Step 2" when ready

"For I know the plans I have for you," declares the Lord, "plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future."

Jeremiah 29:11

Before You Begin

Please read each statement and click to acknowledge your understanding. All three must be checked to proceed.

I understand this is a journey, not a race.

I will take my time with each step and not rush through the content.

I am open to discovering my true identity in Christ.

I will approach this journey with an open heart and mind, ready to receive truth.

I commit to completing all 8 steps of this journey.

I will not abandon this process halfway. I will see it through to the end.
