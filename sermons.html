Hear the Word. Behold Jesus.
Live from What He Has Finished.

LightMode Ministries sermons are being prayerfully prepared to proclaim the truth of God's Word, reveal the heart of the Father, and help believers live confidently from the finished work of Jesus Christ.

Each message will be Scripture-rich, faith-filled, Christ-centered, and created to strengthen understanding, awaken hope, and encourage you in your daily walk with God.

Welcome to the Sermons Library

This space is being prepared to hold messages that proclaim Jesus, strengthen faith, and help God's children see His Word more clearly.

As sermons are completed, they will be added here in video and audio formats so you can receive them at your own pace and return whenever encouragement is needed.

The Heart Behind Every Sermon

Every LightMode sermon will be rooted in Scripture and centered on Jesus Christ.

These messages will not be created merely to inspire for a moment. They will be prayerfully prepared to illuminate truth, reveal the Father's heart, strengthen faith, and help believers live from what Jesus has already accomplished.

The Word of God will remain central. The finished work of Jesus will remain the foundation. And every message will leave the listener looking toward Christ with hope.

Messages Are Being Prepared

New sermons will be added to this space as they are prayerfully developed and recorded.

We are taking the time to prepare each message with care so that every sermon is worthy of remaining in the LightMode Teaching Library for years to come.

Thank you for allowing us to prepare slowly, teach faithfully, and trust the Holy Spirit with the timing of every message.
