Conversation Starters

Not sure how to begin talking about faith with your child? These open-ended questions are designed to spark natural, meaningful dialogue after any episode.

1
What was your favorite part of today's story? Why did it stand out to you?
2
Did the main character remind you of anyone? How would you have acted in their place?
3
What did you learn about God today? How does that change how you see Him?
4
Is there something you're afraid of right now? How can we pray about it together?
5
How can you shine your light this week at school? Let's think of one small way together.
6
What does it mean to be brave for God? Can you think of a time you were brave?

Every Question Matters

There is no perfect way to talk about faith. Your willingness to listen and explore together is what makes the difference.
