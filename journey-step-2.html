Who Are You?

Before you can walk in your true identity, you must first discover the foundation of who you already are in Christ — not who the world says you are.

Created in God's Image

Your identity is not based on what you do, what you have, or what others say about you. Your true identity is rooted in the profound truth that you are created in the image of God.

  • You are fearfully and wonderfully made (Psalm 139:14)
  • You are God's masterpiece (Ephesians 2:10)
  • Your value is intrinsic, not earned

The Truth of Your Identity

Many voices try to define you — culture, past mistakes, even your own thoughts. But God's truth remains unshaken. Today, we choose to believe what He says about you.

  • You are chosen and dearly loved (Colossians 3:12)
  • You are a child of God (John 1:12)
  • Nothing can separate you from His love (Romans 8:38-39)

"See what great love the Father has lavished on us, that we should be called children of God! And that is what we are."

1 John 3:1

Reflect & Respond

Take a moment to journal your thoughts. Who has the world told you that you are? What does God say about you? Write down one truth you want to carry with you today.

Before You Move Forward

Complete each task below to unlock the next step of your journey.

I have read the truth of my identity in Christ.

I understand that my identity is rooted in God's Word, not in the opinions of others.

I have reflected on who God says I am.

I have taken time to journal and consider the truth of my identity as a child of God.

I choose to believe what God says about me.

I will reject the lies of the enemy and embrace my true identity in Christ.
