How to Use Coloring Books

Our coloring books are designed to be more than just fun — they are tools for spiritual growth and family connection.

1

Download & Print

Click the download button to get your free PDF. Print on standard A4 or letter paper.

2

Color Together

Sit with your child and color together. Talk about the story and what it means.

3

Reflect & Pray

Use the conversation starters to discuss the lesson and end with a short prayer.

Create Memories

Every page is an opportunity to grow closer to God and each other.

💡

Parent Tip: Make It a Habit

Set aside 15 minutes each week for coloring and conversation. Use the Parental Guides to dive deeper into each story's meaning and discover discussion questions tailored for your child's age.
