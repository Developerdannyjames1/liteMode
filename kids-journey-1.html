You Are a Child of God!

Before we begin our adventure, let's remember who you are. You are not just any kid — you are a child of the King! God made you, He loves you, and He has a special plan for your life.

What Is This Journey?

This is a special adventure just for you! Each step will help you learn more about God, who you are, and how much He loves you.

  • 6 fun steps to explore
  • Bible verses to learn
  • Fun activities to try

How Does It Work?

Each step has something to read, a Bible verse to remember, and a task to complete. When you finish all the tasks, you'll move to the next step!

  • Read the message
  • Learn the verse
  • Complete the task
