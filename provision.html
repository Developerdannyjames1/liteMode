PRAYER THREE

Provision and
Financial Breakthrough

A prayer for God's provision, financial wisdom, open doors, and breakthrough in every area of need.

READ THE PRAYER
Read the complete prayer below, or follow along as you listen.

Heavenly Father, I come before Your throne of grace, asking for Your provision to cover every need in my life...

SCRIPTURES TO CARRY WITH YOU
Key verses to strengthen your faith and remind you of God's promises.

"And my God will supply every need of yours according to His riches in glory in Christ Jesus." – Philippians 4:19

CONTINUE TO DECLARE
Faith-filled declarations from this prayer to speak over your life.

I declare that God is my Provider. I declare that I lack nothing.

REFLECTION
A moment to reflect, receive, and listen to the Holy Spirit.

Take a moment to be still before the Lord. What is He speaking to your heart today?

YOU ARE NOT ALONE

The God who provides is faithful. He loves you deeply, He is near to you today, and His Word remains faithful.

“The Lord is near to all who call on Him, to all who call on Him in truth.”
– Psalm 145:18

SCRIPTURES TO
CARRY WITH YOU

“And my God will supply every need of yours according to His riches in glory in Christ Jesus.”
– Philippians 4:19
“The Lord is my shepherd; I lack nothing.”
– Psalm 23:1
“Honor the Lord with your wealth, with the firstfruits of all your crops.”
– Proverbs 3:9

CONTINUE TO DECLARE

  • God is my Provider.
  • I lack nothing.
  • Every need of mine is met according to His riches.
  • Doors of provision are open to me.
  • I walk in wisdom with everything He gives me.
  • I receive financial breakthrough in Jesus' name.
  • God's blessings overtake me and my household.
