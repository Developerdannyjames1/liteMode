Family Devotion Time

How to Do Family Devotions

Spending time with God as a family is one of the best gifts you can give your children. Here is a simple way to start.

Read Together

Pick a short Bible story and read it aloud as a family. Let the kids take turns reading.

Talk About It

Ask simple questions. What did you learn? How can we be more like Jesus today?

Pray Together

Hold hands and pray. Let each family member thank God for one thing they are grateful for.

Parent Tips

Guiding Your Family in Faith

Being a spiritual leader in your home is a beautiful calling. Here are some practical tips to help you nurture faith in your children every day.

Lead by Example

Children learn more from what they see than what they hear. Let them see you reading the Bible and praying daily.

Make It Fun

Use songs, games, and creative activities to teach Bible stories. Faith should feel joyful, not like a chore.

Talk Openly

Create a safe space where kids can ask questions about God, faith, and life without fear of judgment.
