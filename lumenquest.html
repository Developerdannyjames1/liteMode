Discover LumenQuest

A Christian Adventure Where Truth Reveals the Way

LumenQuest is a Christian animated adventure series filled with wonder, friendship, prayer, discovery, meaningful challenges, and hopeful restoration. The places the children explore help them see what fear, courage, truth, wisdom, forgiveness, hope, and unity can do. They don't simply hear a lesson—they experience it and choose how to respond.
