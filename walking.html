You Are Not Alone

Jesus walks with you every step of the way. He is your Friend, your Guide, and your Strength. He goes before you, beside you, and within you. You are never walking alone—He is with you always, and His plans for you are good.

SCRIPTURES TO STAND ON

  • John 15:4-5
  • Psalm 23:3
  • Psalm 1:2-3
  • Lamentations 3:22-23
  • Proverbs 3:5-6
  • Micah 6:8
  • Galatians 2:20
  • Romans 8:14
  • 1 Thessalonians 5:16-18
  • Philippians 3:13-14

DECLARE OVER YOUR LIFE

  • I choose to walk closely with Jesus every day.
  • I hear His voice and follow His leading.
  • His love is the foundation of my life.
  • I delight in His Word and obey with joy.
  • I walk in step with the Holy Spirit.
  • I reflect Jesus in all that I do and say.
  • I am empowered to live for God's glory.
  • I fix my eyes on Jesus, the Author and Finisher of my faith.
  • I will not grow weary in doing good.
  • I will finish well, strong in the Lord.
  • Jesus is my Lord, my Life, and my All.
  • I walk daily with Jesus—and He with me.

But as for me, I will always have hope; I will praise You more and more. I will tell of all Your wonderful deeds.

PSALM 71:14
