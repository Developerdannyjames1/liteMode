Who Am I Now?

Because of what Christ has done, you are not who you used to be. You are a new creation. Today, we explore the reality of your transformed identity.

A New Creation

The moment you placed your faith in Christ, something radical happened. You didn't just get a fresh start — you became a completely new person. The old has gone, the new has come.

  • You are a new creation (2 Corinthians 5:17)
  • Your old self was crucified with Christ (Romans 6:6)
  • You have been raised to walk in newness of life (Romans 6:4)

Your New Identity

Your identity is no longer defined by your past, your failures, or what others think of you. You are defined by who God says you are — and He says you are His beloved child.

  • You are a child of God (John 1:12)
  • You are chosen, holy, and dearly loved (Colossians 3:12)
  • You are seated with Christ in heavenly places (Ephesians 2:6)

"Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come: The old has gone, the new is here!"

2 Corinthians 5:17

Declare This Over Your Life

Speak these truths aloud. Let them sink deep into your heart.

"I am a new creation. The old is gone. I am who God says I am."

Reflect & Respond

Take a moment to journal your thoughts. What does it mean to you that you are a new creation? How does your identity in Christ change the way you see yourself?

Before You Move Forward

Complete each task below to unlock the next step of your journey.

I embrace my identity as a new creation.

I understand that I am not who I used to be — I am made new in Christ.

I have declared the truth of my new identity.

I have spoken aloud that I am who God says I am.

I will live from my new identity today.

I choose to walk in the truth of who I am in Christ, not in the lies of the past.
