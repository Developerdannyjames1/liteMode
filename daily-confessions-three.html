Daily Confessions

Speaking God's Truth ... Living From His Finished Work

Let the Word of God fill your heart, renew your mind and shape your day. These Spirit-filled confessions will help you stand in what Jesus has already accomplished so you can live in His love, peace, power and victory.

Daily Confession Three

I Live from Christ's Finished Work

It Is Finished. It Is Mine. I Rest in It.

Scripture Foundation

"It is finished." And bowing His head, He gave up His spirit.

John 19:30 (NKJV)

Speak this confession out loud, slowly and with faith.
Allow God's truth to take root in your heart today.

Today, I Confess ...

  • Jesus has completed everything for me.
    John 19:30 | Hebrews 10:14
  • I am saved by grace through faith, not by my works.
    Ephesians 2:8–9
  • I rest in His finished work, not in my striving.
    Hebrews 4:10
  • Every promise of God is Yes and Amen in Christ.
    2 Corinthians 1:20
  • I am free from guilt, shame and condemnation.
    Romans 8:1–2
  • I walk in victory over sin, fear and the enemy.
    1 Corinthians 15:57
  • I have been reconciled to God through the cross.
    Colossians 1:20–22
  • I live today from what Jesus has already done.
    Galatians 2:20

Christ has finished it —

and I receive it by faith today and always!

Let This Truth Shape Your Day

You do not have to earn what Jesus has finished.
You simply receive it.
You live from it.
You walk in the victory He has already won.

Speak the truth.
Believe the truth.
Walk in the truth.

"For by one offering He has perfected forever those who are being sanctified."

Hebrews 10:14 (NKJV)
