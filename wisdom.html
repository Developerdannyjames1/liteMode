PRAYER FOUR

Wisdom, Guidance,
and Clear Direction

A prayer for divine wisdom, discernment, clear direction, and confidence in God's perfect leading.

Find a quiet place, settle your heart, and join me in prayer.
0:00 / 7:02
READ THE PRAYER
Read the complete prayer below, or follow along as you listen.

Heavenly Father, I come before Your throne of grace, asking for Your divine wisdom and guidance to direct my every step...

SCRIPTURES TO CARRY WITH YOU
Key verses to strengthen your faith and remind you of God's promises.

"If any of you lacks wisdom, you should ask God, who gives generously to all without finding fault, and it will be given to you." – James 1:5

CONTINUE TO DECLARE
Faith-filled declarations from this prayer to speak over your life.

I declare that God gives me wisdom generously. I declare that He directs my steps.

REFLECTION
A moment to reflect, receive, and listen to the Holy Spirit.

Take a moment to be still before the Lord. What is He speaking to your heart today?

YOU ARE NOT ALONE

The God who guides you is faithful. He loves you deeply, He is near to you today, and His Word remains faithful.

“I will instruct you and teach you in the way you should go; I will counsel you with my loving eye on you.”
– Psalm 32:8

CARRY WITH YOU

– James 1:5
“In all your ways acknowledge Him, and He will make your paths straight.”
– Proverbs 3:6
“Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path.”

CONTINUE TO DECLARE

  • God gives me wisdom generously.
  • He directs my steps.
  • I trust His plan for my life.
  • His guidance brings me peace.
  • I hear His voice clearly.
  • He leads me in the right way.
  • I walk in confidence knowing He is with me.
