PRAYER ELEVEN

Thanksgiving,
Praise, and
Remembering
God's Faithfulness

A joyful prayer of gratitude for all that God has done, is doing, and will do. Lift your heart in praise and remember His goodness that never fails.

LISTEN TO THIS PRAYER

18:27
SCRIPTURE FOUNDATION
God's Word that reminds you to give thanks, praise Him, and remember His faithfulness.

Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good; His love endures forever. (Psalm 107:1)

PRAYER
A guided prayer to help you offer thanks and celebrate God's goodness.

Heavenly Father, I come before You with a heart overflowing with gratitude. I thank You for Your faithfulness, Your love, and Your endless goodness...

DECLARE AND BELIEVE
Speak these declarations of faith and stand on God's promises with joy and gratitude.

I declare that I give thanks to the Lord with all my heart. His love and faithfulness endure forever. I will praise Him in every season of my life.

JOURNAL AND REFLECT
Write your prayers, memories, and thank you notes to God for all He has done.

Take time to write down the ways God has been faithful in your life. Reflect on His goodness and let gratitude fill your heart.

You Are Not Alone

God has been good to you, and He will continue to be good. He holds every moment of your life in His hands. When you look back, you will see His hand of favor. When you look ahead, you can trust His perfect plans. Give Him praise—He is so worthy!

DECLARE OVER YOUR HEART
I give thanks to the Lord with all my heart.
His love and faithfulness endures forever.
I will praise Him in every season of my life.
I remember His goodness and His miracles.
I trust Him for what He is doing next.
I speak blessings over my life and my family.
My heart is filled with joy and gratitude.
His faithfulness is my firm foundation.
I will recount His goodness to others.
I choose a grateful heart every day.
Enter His gates with thanksgiving and His courts with praise; give thanks to Him and praise His name.
